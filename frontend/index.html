क्यों ज़रूरी है · WHY IT MATTERS

Planning without history is guesswork

०१

New wards

A newly formed ward has no complaint record, yet it still needs municipal field staff and budget from day one.

०२

Missing records

Some wards never logged their historical issues, so their real demand stays invisible to municipal commissioners.

०३

Staff and budget

City corporations handle thousands of calls. An equitable split of workers and budget requires an objective model for every ward.

कैसे काम करता है · HOW IT WORKS

From ward attributes to a demand map in four steps

1

Ward attributes

The details your dataset holds for each ward, such as population, area, and type of locality.

2

Map data & location

Roads, markets, drains and the wards next door, taken from the user's location and map coordinates.

3

Regression model

Learns from wards that do have complaint records, then estimates demand for wards that do not.

4

Demand dashboard

A ward-by-ward map showing predicted complaints, a likely range, and suggested field staff allocation.

विशेषताएँ · FEATURES

More than a single number per ward

Demand by complaint type

See how much of a ward's forecast is roads, garbage, water or streetlights, so the right municipal team is dispatched.

Confidence for every ward

Each estimate comes with a likely range and a High, Medium or Low label, so city planners know where to double-check.

Location & dataset dynamic

Upload any city dataset (CSV/JSON) or detect your live GPS location to recalculate civic allocations instantly.
